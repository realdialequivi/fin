How Fin stays compliant

Built for the line between helpful and legal advice.

Off The Record is a technology platform, not a law firm. Fin operates inside the same boundary — with explicit guardrails reviewed and tunable by your team.

Approved KB only

Fin answers exclusively from content your team has authored and approved. No hallucinated facts, no scraped sources.

UPL-aware escalation

Requests for case-specific legal advice are detected and routed to the assigned attorney — never answered.

Jurisdiction-aware

State and city carve-outs (NJ, NYC, CDL programs) are encoded as KB rules. Fin follows them automatically.

Auditable by design

Every Fin response is logged with the KB source it pulled from — so legal and ops can review at any time.
